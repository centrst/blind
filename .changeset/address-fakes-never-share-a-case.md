Two different addresses no longer get two letter cases of one fake. A second address at a host that already had a fake, met in a later call or drawing the same word by chance, used to get the first address's fake in capitals. A reader took the two for one mailbox, and a reply that wrote the fake in another case came back as the wrong address. Each address now gets a fake of its own, numbered when a host has more addresses than there are words to draw. One address written in two letter cases still gets two cases of one fake.
