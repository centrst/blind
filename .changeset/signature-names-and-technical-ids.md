A sign-off name is found one blank line below its closing (`Kind regards,`, a blank line, `Priya Raman`) and with a job title after a comma (`Odette Ashworth, CEO`), and the company line under it is found as before. Both used to go out as written.

Technical text is no longer replaced as an id. A lower-case git SHA, a UUID, a hex colour after `#`, a value marked as a SKU (`SKU-4417-BLK`), and a lower-case model, release or file name made of words, version pieces and dates (`gpt-4o-2024-08-06`, `claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022`, `release-2026-01-15`, `src/v2024/parser1234.ts`) now stay as written. They used to become look-alikes such as `tci-7a-2283-14-27`. Order references in capitals, a lower-case one whose number is not a date, digits after `#`, product codes without the SKU mark and every typed id (cards, SSN, IBAN, DNI, NIE, CIF, VAT) are replaced as before.
